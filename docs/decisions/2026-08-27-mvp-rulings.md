# LeetVibeCode MVP — implementation decision record

Eighty-four rulings made while executing `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-08-27-leetvibecode-mvp.md`
against `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-22-leetvibecode-mvp-design.md`.

A **ruling** is a decision made where the plan, the spec and the code disagreed, or where a
review found something the plan had not anticipated. Each says what was decided, why, and —
where it was recorded — what it costs if the decision turns out to be wrong.

R1–R20 came from a pre-flight scan of the whole plan against the spec, before any code was
written. R21 onward came from implementation and review.

Several rulings changed after they were made, because a reviewer or an implementer tested the
reasoning instead of accepting it: **R62** was amended, **R65** was reversed outright, **R72**
was rewritten twice, and **R14**'s stated rationale was invalidated by a later task while its
conclusion survived. Those histories are included rather than tidied away.

Entries list every mention of a ruling, so a ruling that was later amended, endorsed or
contradicted shows that history rather than only its final state.
